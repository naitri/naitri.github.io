PolarDepth received the Best Poster Award at the Insect-scale Autonomy Workshop and Best Poster Runner-Up at the Bridging Perspectives in Navigation: Lessons Learned and Future Directions Workshop, both held with IROS 2026 in Pittsburgh. PolarDepth uses a single polarization camera to recover depth around glass and other reflective surfaces, where color-only methods fail. UMIACS featured the awards in a news story on 7 October 2026.
