Naitri Rajyaguru

Insect-inspired AI for tiny robots.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, advised by Prof. Yiannis Aloimonos. A honeybee crosses miles of changing terrain, finds food, and returns home on roughly a million neurons. I build insect-inspired AI on those terms: compact representations that keep only what a robot needs in order to act, so palm-sized robots can navigate on the power, memory, and compute they actually carry.

RingNav: Task-Shaped Circular Memory for Visual Heading

Under review Paper ↗Website ↗Video ↗

Naitri Rajyaguru*, Aryan Tajne*, Stephen Jardim, Cornelia Fermüller, Yiannis Aloimonos * equal contribution

A 10.6k-parameter visual heading estimator that keeps heading as a state on the circle and learns only the turn between frames, running onboard at 27.3 Hz on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2W and 33.4 Hz on a GAP8 processor.

▶

PolarDepth: Polarization-Guided Monocular Depth for Visual Odometry

IEEE RA-L 2026IROS 2026 Paper ↗Website ↗Video ↗

Naitri Rajyaguru, Tianfu Wang, Aryan Tajne, Botao He, Jiayi Wu, Cornelia Fermüller, Christopher Metzler, Yiannis Aloimonos

A single polarization camera recovers cues that RGB and active depth sensors often miss around glass, improving monocular depth and visual odometry in glass-dominant environments.

CodedVO: Coded Visual Odometry

IEEE RA-L 2024ICRA40 Paper ↗arXiv ↗Website ↗

Naitri Rajyaguru*, Sachin Shah*, Chahat Deep Singh, Christopher Metzler, Yiannis Aloimonos * equal contribution

A coded aperture physically encodes metric depth cues into images to resolve scale ambiguity in monocular visual odometry.
